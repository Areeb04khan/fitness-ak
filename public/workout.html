Barbell Bench Press

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Feet flat on floor, back arched slightly, shoulder blades retracted
  • Grip: Hands slightly wider than shoulder-width, wrists neutral
  • Descent: 2 seconds down, lower to mid-chest, elbows ~45° from body
  • Pause: Brief pause at bottom, no bouncing
  • Drive: Explosive press up, lock out elbows at top
  • Breathing: Inhale on descent, exhale on press
REST: 2 minutes

Dumbbell Incline Press

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Bench inclined 30–45°, feet on floor, back against pad
  • Grip: Dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward
  • Path: Press up and slightly inward, following natural arc
  • Range: Full range of motion, dumbbells near shoulders at bottom
  • Control: 1–2 sec eccentric (lowering), squeeze at top
REST: 90 seconds

Barbell Curl

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Feet shoulder-width apart, bar at hip level, knees slightly bent
  • Grip: Hands ~shoulder-width, underhand grip (palms facing away)
  • Movement: Curl bar toward shoulders, lead with elbows
  • Elbows: Keep elbows stationary (no swinging), tucked to sides
  • Squeeze: 1–2 sec pause at top, bicep fully contracted
  • Eccentric: Slow 2-sec lower, control the weight down
  • No momentum: Strict form, no body sway
REST: 90 seconds

Tricep Rope Pushdown

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Face cable machine, rope attachment at upper chest height
  • Grip: Grab rope, elbows bent 90°, tucked to sides
  • Movement: Push rope down until elbows fully extended
  • Squeeze: 1 sec squeeze at bottom, full tricep contraction
  • Return: Controlled return to start, resist the pull up
  • Elbows: Keep stationary throughout, only forearms move
REST: 60 seconds

Barbell Back Squat

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Bar on upper back (not neck), feet shoulder-width apart, toes ~10° out
  • Core: Chest up, shoulders back, spine neutral
  • Descent: 2 seconds down, knees tracking over toes
  • Depth: Hips drop below knees (full depth squat)
  • Weight: Heels stay on floor, weight in midfoot
  • Drive: Explosive drive up, knees and hips extend together
  • Lockout: Full hip and knee extension at top
REST: 2 minutes

Leg Press (Machine)

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Sit with back and head on pad, feet on platform shoulder-width apart
  • Grip: Hands on handles for stability
  • Range: Lower until knees ~90°, do NOT lock knees out at top
  • Descent: Controlled lowering, 2 seconds down
  • Drive: Push through heels, extend knees and hips
  • Knees: Keep knees tracking over toes, no caving inward
REST: 90 seconds (varies by exercise)

Leg Extension (Machine)

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Sit upright, knees bent 90°, bar pad above ankles
  • Movement: Extend knees until fully straight
  • Pause: 1 sec pause at full extension, squeeze quadriceps
  • Return: Slow controlled return, do NOT let weight stack touch
  • Range: Full range of motion, full contraction and stretch
REST: 45–60 seconds

Standing Calf Raise

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Stand upright, hold dumbbell or use machine, feet shoulder-width apart
  • Position: Weight on balls of feet, heels can start on ground or platform
  • Rise: Push through balls of feet, rise up onto toes
  • Peak: Full calf contraction at top, stand on toes
  • Pause: 1 sec pause at top for maximum contraction
  • Lower: Controlled descent, heel touches ground at bottom
REST: 45 seconds

Barbell Bent-Over Row

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Feet shoulder-width, knees slightly bent, hinge at hips ~45°
  • Grip: Hands slightly wider than shoulder-width, overhand grip
  • Chest: Keep chest up, neutral spine, shoulders retracted
  • Pull: Row bar to lower chest, lead with elbows
  • Scapula: Retract shoulder blades, squeeze back muscles at top
  • Control: 2 sec eccentric (lowering), pause at chest
  • Breathing: Exhale on pull, inhale on lower
REST: 90 seconds

Lat Pulldown (Machine)

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Sit upright, knees under thigh pad for stability
  • Grip: Hands ~shoulder-width or slightly wider, overhand grip
  • Pull: Pull bar down to upper chest (clavicle area)
  • Elbows: Drive elbows down and back, not straight down
  • Retraction: Retract shoulder blades, squeeze lats at bottom
  • Control: Slow eccentric (lowering), full range of motion
  • Avoid: Do not lean back excessively or use momentum
REST: 75 seconds

Dumbbell Bicep Curl

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Stand upright, dumbbells at sides, palms facing forward
  • Elbows: Keep elbows stationary, tucked to ribs
  • Curl: Curl dumbbells toward shoulders, lead with elbows not wrists
  • Peak: Full bicep contraction at top, dumbbells near shoulders
  • Squeeze: 1–2 sec pause, feel the squeeze in biceps
  • Eccentric: Slow 2-sec lowering, control the weight
  • No momentum: Strict form, no body sway or cheating
REST: 75 seconds

Face Pulls (Cable)

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Face cable machine, rope attachment at upper face height
  • Grip: Grab rope, arms extended, slight tension in cable
  • Pull: Pull rope toward face, split rope apart at the end
  • Elbows: Elbows high and wide, external shoulder rotation
  • Squeeze: Pause at face, squeeze rear shoulders and upper back
  • Return: Controlled return to start, full stretch
  • Reps: Light weight, high reps (12–15) for shoulder health
REST: 45 seconds

Dumbbell Thrusters

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Hold dumbbells at shoulders, feet shoulder-width apart
  • Squat: Squat down, keep chest up, knees tracking over toes
  • Drive: Explosive drive from squat, as you stand press dumbbells overhead
  • Press: Complete the press at full standing height
  • Lockout: Full arm extension overhead, dumbbells locked out
  • Return: Lower dumbbells to shoulders as you squat down
  • Speed: Fast, powerful, full-body movement — used for fat loss
CIRCUIT EXERCISE: 20–30 sec rest after all exercises

Machine Chest Press

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Sit upright on machine, back flat against pad
  • Handles: Hands on handles, arms bent ~90°
  • Press: Drive handles forward and slightly inward
  • Lockout: Extend arms, do not lock elbows fully out
  • Control: Slow controlled return to start
  • Range: Full range of motion, chest feel the stretch at bottom
CIRCUIT EXERCISE: 20–30 sec rest after all exercises

Barbell Military Press (Standing)

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Stand upright, feet shoulder-width, bar at shoulder height
  • Grip: Hands slightly wider than shoulders, elbows ~90°
  • Core: Brace core, glutes tight, neutral spine
  • Press: Explosive drive bar overhead
  • Lockout: Full arm extension overhead, bar directly over shoulders
  • Eccentric: 2 sec controlled lower to shoulders
  • No sway: Minimal body movement, legs stable
REST: 90 seconds

Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Sit on bench (or stand), dumbbells at shoulder height
  • Palms: Palms facing forward, elbows slightly in front of shoulders
  • Press: Press dumbbells overhead, following natural arc
  • Path: Hands travel up and slightly inward toward midline
  • Lockout: Full arm extension, dumbbells directly overhead
  • Control: Slow eccentric, 1–2 sec pause at top
REST: 75 seconds

Lateral Raise (Dumbbell)

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Stand upright, dumbbells at sides, slight bend in elbows
  • Raise: Raise dumbbells to side, elbows leading (bend stays constant)
  • Height: Raise until dumbbells ~shoulder height (or just below)
  • Squeeze: 1 sec pause at top, feel middle deltoid contraction
  • Lower: Slow controlled descent, full stretch at bottom
  • Wrists: Slight outward rotation, thumbs pointing down/back at top
  • Light weight: This is a shoulder isolation exercise, use light weight
REST: 60 seconds

Rope Tricep Extension (Overhead)

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Face cable machine, rope attachment overhead
  • Grip: Grab rope, step forward, arms bent ~90°, hands overhead
  • Position: Upper arms nearly vertical, elbows in front of head
  • Extend: Extend arms until elbows fully straightened
  • Squeeze: 1 sec squeeze at full extension, triceps fully contracted
  • Lower: Slow controlled return, bend elbows to 90°
  • Stretch: Feel full range of motion from stretch to contraction
REST: 60 seconds

Leg Curl (Machine)

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Lie face down on machine, legs extended, pad behind heels
  • Grip: Hold onto handles for stability
  • Curl: Curl legs up, bringing heels toward glutes
  • Peak: Full hamstring contraction at top, heels near glutes
  • Squeeze: 1 sec pause at top, squeeze hamstrings
  • Lower: Slow controlled lower, full stretch at bottom
  • Range: Full range of motion, no jerky movements
REST: 60 seconds

Bulgarian Split Squat (Dumbbell)

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Stand with back leg elevated on bench, front foot forward
  • Distance: Front foot ~2 feet from bench (adjust for comfort)
  • Dumbbells: Hold dumbbells at sides for added weight
  • Descent: Lower body straight down, front knee bends to ~90°
  • Depth: Back knee nearly touches ground, front shin vertical
  • Drive: Push through front heel, stand back up
  • Reps: Complete all reps on one leg, then switch
REST: 75 seconds

Seated Calf Raise

Form Cues:

  • Setup: Sit on machine or bench, knees bent ~90°, feet on platform
  • Weight: Weight plate on knees or use machine pad
  • Rise: Push through balls of feet, rise onto toes
  • Peak: Full calf contraction at top, stand on toes
  • Pause: 1 sec pause at top for maximum contraction
  • Lower: Controlled descent, heel touches ground at bottom
  • Range: Full range of motion from stretch to peak contraction
REST: 45 seconds
